Grown-up guide

Make maths part of the day.

Muchale Number Garden is designed for short, positive practice. Follow the child’s curiosity, celebrate effort, and stop while it still feels fun.

Simple routineAge pathsOur approach
A simple routine

Five minutes is enough

No pressure, just practice

1️⃣

Choose a path

Pick the age level that feels comfortable, not the one that sounds impressive.

2️⃣

Try together

Ask “How did you think about it?” and let fingers, objects, or drawings help.

3️⃣

Celebrate effort

Notice persistence and ideas, not only correct answers.

Age paths

What each level practises

  • Age 3:  counting, next numbers, and small groups to 5
  • Age 4:  number bonds and take-away stories to 10
  • Age 5:  numbers to 15, equal groups, and fair sharing
  • Age 6:  numbers to 20, mixed operations, and comparisons
  • Class 1–2:  number fluency, place value, and operations to 100
  • Class 3–4:  larger calculations, multiplication, division, and strategies
  • Class 5–7:  confident reasoning with larger numbers and multiple methods
